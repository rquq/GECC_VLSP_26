# Dataset and model guidelines

Sources: the local `VLSP2026-ViGEC-UserAgreement.docx` in alpha/test, and https://aihub.ml/competitions/1038 (checked 2026-10-13).

- Corpus use is limited to NLP/information-retrieval/document-understanding research and development, with no commercial use. Modification for research is permitted; redistribution of original or edited corpus resources is prohibited. Keep corpus, derived examples, repair overlays, and text-bearing outputs private. Published summaries must not reconstruct the corpus. Acknowledge VLSP and VGEC organizers in research reports.
- Competition 1038 permits external pretrained models and data, but requires documentation and access for organizers upon request. Every component and every synthetic generator must have at most 8 billion parameters. Count the unquantized architecture and any adapters; NF4/GGUF storage does not change parameter count. Record model name, immutable revision, exact count and generation settings. Do not use larger cloud/chat models to author training samples for this track.
- Check each generator's license as well as the competition's size rule. SeaLLMs-v3 links a license whose Section 5(b) restricts improving other LLMs with its Materials or outputs. Exclude it from synthetic authoring, correction or verification for Sailor/Qwen training. Direct SeaLLMs fine-tuning is a separate candidate use, subject to its license/notices and corpus restrictions; see `agents/experiments/REPLACEMENT_REVIEW.md`.
- Split original data first with the existing grouped seed-42 protocol. The 81-row holdout stays unchanged. Public/private test inputs are inference-only. Derive repair/generation contexts exclusively from training families. Keep parents, source duplicates, corrected counterparts and template families together for any internal split.
- Keep original IDs, source, label and target in a private overlay. Each proposed change needs evidence, reviewer/model provenance, confidence and review status. Flags are candidates for inspection, not authoritative relabeling. Ellipsis completion may copy an identifiable original span; do not invent omitted content. Preserve uncertain rows in quarantine. A contradictory annotation can justify review without proving an alternative subtype.
- Follow the supplied taxonomy: 0 clean, 1 sentence structure, 2 phrase structure, 3 function words, 4 punctuation. Reject candidates whose effect is primarily spelling, vocabulary, semantic changes, stylistic preference, or multiple competing error types. A correction target must be a complete sentence, not an explanation of the edit.
- Every synthetic pair needs its mechanism, parent/source origin, generator revision/count, prompt version, seed, generation output hash, review evidence and final status. Include matched clean controls. Check numbers, named entities, meaning, and minimal edits. Teacher self-agreement is insufficient evidence of correctness.
- Codex may inspect existing references, prepare deterministic source-span copies, and filter eligible-teacher outputs. New synthetic source/target wording and inferred replacement labels must come from an eligible local teacher or documented human adjudication; analytical review alone is not a claim of human validation. Never rewrite a failed teacher candidate with an uncounted larger assistant and then call it eligible teacher data.
- Curation decisions are versioned in bundle provenance. Legacy substring-based sentence-correctness exclusions remain reproducible for pinned data; new policy v2 requires an explicit sentence-correctness claim. A procedural annotation containing đúng does not establish that the source is clean. Any policy/data promotion requires a new manifest and recipe hashes; do not reinterpret old bundles silently.
- Audit duplicates and near duplicates, train/holdout collisions, class counts, parent counts and mechanism diversity. Keep quality caps and provenance validation; revise policy/version deliberately if needed. Synthetic influence must be controlled rather than increased until the dataset dominates originals.
- Compare one change at a time using training-only internal validation for screening. Report per-class recall/F1, Macro-F1, Micro-F1 and confusion counts; report error-only correction and clean preservation alongside all-row GLEU+. Repeated selection on 81 holdout rows is optimistic evidence, not an independent test.
- Log every experiment under `agents/experiments/`, including rejected changes and failures. Store code/aggregate summaries in Git; store corpus-bearing evidence locally. Update dataset hashes and manifests only when a reviewed version is promoted. Do not claim score improvement from syntax validation or a tiny pipeline smoke.

- Reviewed v4 datasets preserve the full original snapshot and exact holdout, validate each accepted repair against its original row/evidence/eligible blind review, and allow under-cap synthesis without filler. Correction-only preservation of the pinned legacy augmentation requires an exact byte/digest import and its unchanged strict v3 validation; it does not authorize bypassing review/diversity checks for new samples. Do not remove augmentation wholesale when it would leave only a handful of usable rare-type corrections.
- Record negative pilot/model results and save/reload failures with their recovery. Keep equivalent inference precision on both sides of reload checks. Different data sizes at fixed epochs give different optimizer-step budgets; report that confound before claiming that cleaning or augmentation caused a score change.

## Round-trip translation review — 2026-10-14

The user requested an unchanged validation-only export and a check of Vietnamese→pivot language→Vietnamese augmentation. The competition1038 page allows synthetic generation pipelines under the8B limit; interpreting that to include round-trip translation is an inference, since it does not explicitly name this method. Every translator in both directions and any model generating error examples or new labels must be verifiably within the cap. Record each model revision/count/license, translation directions, prompts, settings, parent family and output hashes. The corpus agreement permits research modifications but prohibits redistribution of edited corpus resources; keep translated derivatives private.

GPT-6.1 Sol's official model page gives reasoning settings but no parameter count. Its ≤8B eligibility is therefore unverified; do not use it to translate, paraphrase, author examples or infer replacement training labels for1038. Extra-high reasoning is an effort setting, not evidence of a compliant architecture size. Codex can build tools and analyze existing evidence; it must not supply uncounted teacher wording or labels. This is an eligibility conclusion from unavailable size evidence, not a claim about Sol's exact architecture.

The metadata-only NLLB-200-distilled-600M audit found615073792 native parameters, revision f8d333a098d19b4fd9a8b18f94170487ad3f821d, license CC-BY-NC-4.0. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct is another eligible candidate under the existing native audit. Neither has generated new augmentation for this request; model suitability and output quality remain to be measured.

Use only training parents for augmentation. The separate81-row export is evaluation-only, not a generation seed pool. Round trips can remove the original error or change its type; never automatically inherit an original error label. A useful pilot would translate reviewed clean/complete training references, verify meaning and fluency, then construct one supported taxonomy error with a ≤8B model and review it with matched clean controls. Preserve parent groups and uncertain candidates. Model-generated agreement alone does not establish correctness.

The81 rows are our local holdout taken from organizer training data, not an official public/private test set. Their training exclusion is the current project evaluation protocol, not an additional organizer rule. A later final fit using all organizer training rows would need a documented protocol change and could no longer report independent validation on these same81 rows. Official public/private test inputs remain inference-only.
