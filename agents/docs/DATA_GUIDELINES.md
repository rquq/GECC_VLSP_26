# Dataset and model guidelines

Sources: the local `VLSP2026-ViGEC-UserAgreement.docx` in alpha/test, and https://aihub.ml/competitions/1038 (checked 2026-10-13).

- Corpus use is limited to NLP/information-retrieval/document-understanding research and development, with no commercial use. Modification for research is permitted; redistribution of original or edited corpus resources is prohibited. Keep corpus, derived examples, repair overlays, and text-bearing outputs private. Published summaries must not reconstruct the corpus. Acknowledge VLSP and VGEC organizers in research reports.
- Competition 1038 permits external pretrained models and data, but requires documentation and access for organizers upon request. Every component and every synthetic generator must have at most 8 billion parameters. Count the unquantized architecture and any adapters; NF4/GGUF storage does not change parameter count. Record model name, immutable revision, exact count and generation settings. Do not use larger cloud/chat models to author training samples for this track.
- Split original data first with the existing grouped seed-42 protocol. The 81-row holdout stays unchanged. Public/private test inputs are inference-only. Derive repair/generation contexts exclusively from training families. Keep parents, source duplicates, corrected counterparts and template families together for any internal split.
- Keep original IDs, source, label and target in a private overlay. Each proposed change needs evidence, reviewer/model provenance, confidence and review status. Flags are candidates for inspection, not authoritative relabeling. Ellipsis completion may copy an identifiable original span; do not invent omitted content. Preserve uncertain rows in quarantine. A contradictory annotation can justify review without proving an alternative subtype.
- Follow the supplied taxonomy: 0 clean, 1 sentence structure, 2 phrase structure, 3 function words, 4 punctuation. Reject candidates whose effect is primarily spelling, vocabulary, semantic changes, stylistic preference, or multiple competing error types. A correction target must be a complete sentence, not an explanation of the edit.
- Every synthetic pair needs its mechanism, parent/source origin, generator revision/count, prompt version, seed, generation output hash, review evidence and final status. Include matched clean controls. Check numbers, named entities, meaning, and minimal edits. Teacher self-agreement is insufficient evidence of correctness.
- Audit duplicates and near duplicates, train/holdout collisions, class counts, parent counts and mechanism diversity. Keep quality caps and provenance validation; revise policy/version deliberately if needed. Synthetic influence must be controlled rather than increased until the dataset dominates originals.
- Compare one change at a time using training-only internal validation for screening. Report per-class recall/F1, Macro-F1, Micro-F1 and confusion counts; report error-only correction and clean preservation alongside all-row GLEU+. Repeated selection on 81 holdout rows is optimistic evidence, not an independent test.
- Log every experiment under `agents/experiments/`, including rejected changes and failures. Store code/aggregate summaries in Git; store corpus-bearing evidence locally. Update dataset hashes and manifests only when a reviewed version is promoted. Do not claim score improvement from syntax validation or a tiny pipeline smoke.
